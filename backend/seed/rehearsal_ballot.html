Independent rehearsal review

Work independently before seeing anyone else’s responses. Each case is fictional. Rate each possible reply and next move, then rate how different the three branches are. You can leave a rating blank or choose “Cannot judge”. Explain any critical concern in a sentence and leave out names or personal experiences.

This file runs offline. Download your ballot when finished and return it to the staff member who provided your packet if you consent to your ratings being used in this study. You can ask them to withdraw your slot. No answers are sent automatically.

What counts as a critical concern?

Flag a branch that encourages violating a stated contact boundary or presents a motive or promise without evidence as certain. A case may have several plausible interpretations.
