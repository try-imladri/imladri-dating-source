# Sixteen TikTok videos for Imladri

Use vertical 9:16 video, readable captions, a quiet background, and a visible product name. Approximate running times are 25–40 seconds at a natural pace. Use an original voice recording and original or properly licensed visuals. Show the on-screen label through the whole relevant scene, not only the first frame. Enable the appropriate commercial disclosure. Policy details and sources are in [editorial rules](editorial-and-platform-rules.md).

All CTAs below match the founding intake state. If the local pool becomes available, change the final line only after confirming the current product experience. Do not ask viewers to disclose age, orientation, intimate answers, or private dating histories in public comments.

## 01 · The planner and the spontaneous person

**Format:** One adult actor playing two fictional people; calendar prop. **Label:** Fictional dating scenario. **Length:** 30 seconds.

**0–3s, hook:** “You both want another date. So why are the plans already stressful?”

**3–18s, scene:** Planner: “Can we pick Saturday now?” Spontaneous person: “Can we see how the week goes?” Narrator: “One wants certainty. One wants room. That exchange alone doesn’t tell you who cares more.”

**18–28s, useful turn:** “Try: ‘I like seeing you. Could we save Saturday, then choose what we do on Friday?’ Ask how they like to make plans before deciding what their timing means.”

## Reusable reply language

- “Are these real profiles?” → “The profiles in our walkthroughs are fictional demos. We’re recruiting the founding community; availability depends on who joins locally.”
- “Does a score prove we fit?” → “No. Preferences can start a useful conversation. They don’t prove chemistry or predict a relationship.”
- “Do I have to answer private questions?” → “No. Intimacy questions are optional. Read the separate matching and sharing choices in enrollment.”
- “Where can I join?” → “The link in our bio opens Imladri. Please enter location details privately there rather than in comments.”
- “What does it cost?” → “Dating founding enrollment is free, with no card required. Existing private-tool memberships are separate.”

These are response templates, not messages to send automatically. Move account or privacy issues to the support route; do not request sensitive details in comments or direct messages.
