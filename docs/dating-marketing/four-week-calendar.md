# Four-week founding community campaign

This is a proposed operating cadence, not a forecast. Launch dates stay relative until the product, city, and operating coverage are ready. Use the sixteen numbered scripts in [tiktok-scripts.md](tiktok-scripts.md). Publish four times a week, then use the remaining days for recording, comment review, product corrections, and recruitment follow-up. Pick posting times from the account’s actual audience data; there is no assumed universal best time.

## Alex’s local recruitment work

Choose one city where Alex can make introductions. Prepare a factual one-page invitation: who the founding beta is for, what enrollment asks for, how private choices work, and how availability depends on the local pool. Identify communities and creators whose adult audience already attends local events or wants intentional dating. Record a named contact, audience area, adult-audience fit, proposed activity, introduction status, and consent to follow up. Do not scrape dating profiles or buy intimate audience lists.

Start with a proposed goal of ten qualified community conversations per week; it is a work target, not a guaranteed conversion forecast. A creator partner must use the actual product, label promotional content, and avoid claiming dates or results they have not experienced. Cash, free services, and other incentives need the appropriate disclosure. Negotiate permission to reuse footage; an organic collaboration does not automatically grant advertising rights. No outreach or posting is authorized merely by this document.

## Measurement

Use one non-sensitive campaign identifier per creative, such as `tt-w1-v01`. Only put that identifier into an approved attribution mechanism. Never put age, orientation, intimacy answers, matching preferences, city-level sensitive cohorts, profile IDs, email, or consent values into URL parameters, advertising pixels, or external analytics events. Imladri’s present public CTA uses a broad origin; per-video attribution requires an explicit implementation or an aggregated signup-source question. Do not assume those events already exist.

| Funnel stage | Definition | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video view / completion | Platform’s reported measure, with its definition retained | Creative attention, not product demand |
| Landing visit | An aggregate, non-sensitive visit attributable to a creative | Does the video explain an offer people want to inspect? |
| Qualified adult enrollment | Consent-complete adult signup in the area the team can support | Demand from people the first community could serve |
| Completed profile | Dating profile has required fields and accepted publication choices | Onboarding value and friction |
| Reviewed active profile | Approved, opted into discovery, and active in a defined recent window | Usable community supply |
| Reciprocal pool coverage | Share of active people who have at least the agreed number of mutually eligible, active candidates | Does a local community exist for the actual preferences? |
| Mutual interest | New reciprocal likes per eligible active person | Interest beyond signup |
| Two-way conversation | Both participants send a message in a new match | A more meaningful interaction than a match count |
| Date planned / held | Separately optional self-report, never inferred from message text | Intended outcome, reported honestly with response rate |
| Paid conversion / retention | Verified payment and return activity only when a paid dating offer exists | Economic evidence, not hypothetical willingness |

Report numerator, denominator, time window, and cohort age. A signup rate is qualified enrollments / attributed visits. Profile completion is completed profiles / consent-complete enrollments. Reciprocal coverage is eligible active people above the agreed candidate threshold / all active people in the cohort. A reported date rate must also disclose the share who answered the optional question. Tiny groups should be suppressed from shared reports rather than exposed as individual counts.

Do not count seeded examples, staff accounts, test photos, or synthetic profiles as community supply. Public figures and Your Type portraits are not members. Age attestation, photo review, and identity verification are distinct checks; report exactly what was performed.

## Weekly decisions

These are proposed decision rules to agree before launch, not industry benchmarks:

1. **First week:** Fix broken links, consent confusion, photo upload issues, or misleading copy immediately. Use attention metrics to learn which hook is understood; do not select a winner from a handful of visits.
2. **Second week:** Compare creatives after the same observation window. If visits fail to become complete profiles, inspect onboarding with real participants before buying more traffic.
3. **Third week:** Inspect reciprocal coverage inside the smallest useful private cohorts. Example test: at least five mutually eligible, recently active profiles for most of a cohort. That threshold is a hypothesis to evaluate, not a promise shown to users. Recruit where the pool has gaps; do not send people irrelevant candidates to inflate activity.
4. **Fourth week:** Continue if the community has usable reciprocal coverage and growing two-way conversations. Run a paid-offer experiment only after participants have experienced real introductions and purchase/cancellation is operational. A proposed $19 monthly price is a test value, not a live product promise or validated optimum.

If demand is scattered across locations, narrow recruitment further. If people enroll but do not find reciprocal interest, improve the local mix and proposition before adding a second branch. If there are useful conversations but low return, ask consenting participants what stopped them. Expand only when there is evidence for a repeatable local experience.

## Paid promotion gate

Organic posting and paid dating advertising have different rules. Do not boost these posts automatically. Before any paid dating campaign, confirm the market permits dating ads, contact TikTok’s Sales Representative, meet the required 18+ targeting, confirm model ages/appearance and creative eligibility, and review the landing page. No paid budget is committed by this kit. See [official-policy sources and public-figure rules](editorial-and-platform-rules.md).
