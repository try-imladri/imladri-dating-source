# Archetypes, public figures, and TikTok production rules

Policy review: 19 November 2026. Recheck the linked official policy before recording or buying distribution because platform requirements can change. These are operating rules for this campaign, not a substitute for reviewing a specific licensing agreement or legal question.

## Lead with fictional situations and the real product

Use the first sixteen videos as written: eight fictional scenarios, four labeled demonstrations, four founder/community/privacy posts. Archetypes are a creative device for a recognizable situation, such as planning ahead or warming up slowly. Avoid a rigid personality verdict, a diagnosis, or a claim that two types predict love. Show the uncertainty and a question someone could actually ask.

Public-figure videos should be a small later experiment after product and local-community videos have measurable results. They can attract attention without attracting people who can use the service. They are not the primary acquisition strategy, and an endorsement cannot be invented. Keep `/figures` in the editorial Learn area rather than treating recognizable faces as member inventory.

## Permitted public-figure format

Use a documented public example: a published interview about working methods, a publicly stated communication preference, or a clearly identified fictional performance. Attribute the original source on screen or in the caption and distinguish the public example from an interpretation. For instance:

> “In this published interview, [person] describes preparing carefully for work. That public example raises a dating question: how much planning do you like? We’re discussing the interview, not claiming to know their private relationships.”

This is a template requiring a verified source and rights review, not a completed script about a named person. Quote sparingly, obtain necessary permission, and retain the original source URL, access date, exact excerpt, asset owner, and reuse/license evidence with the production file. Use licensed or original footage, or text-only commentary where appropriate; a publicly viewable clip is not automatically licensed for commercial reuse.

Do not infer or assign a living person’s sexual preferences, intimate boundaries, sexual orientation, hidden motives, diagnosis, attachment disorder, or ideal partner from appearances or public work. Do not label them a match for a member or say “the app knows their type.” Do not suggest they joined, used, endorsed, invested in, or partnered with Imladri without documented authorization. Do not place a public figure’s face on a dating profile, fabricate a quote, clone their voice, or create a synthetic endorsement. A disclaimer does not fix a deceptive core impression.

## Demonstration and privacy rules

- Label every synthetic profile or staged exchange **Fictional demo · not a member** for the duration of the scene.
- Capture an isolated, currently working demonstration flow. Don’t render a mockup as a feature that already works.
- Use original synthetic adult portraits only where allowed by the actual product. Your Type portraits are preference-exploration material, never a stockpile of available people.
- Real customer testimonials require specific permission for the exact claim, footage, duration, and channels. No such testimonial is supplied in this kit.
- Do not record real questionnaire histories, private messages, health information, sexual preferences, precise location, billing information, or admin data.
- Keep intimacy promotion focused on optional participation and control. Don’t show sexual questions, answers, or suggestive imagery as an attention hook.
- Actors must be adults. For paid dating creative, TikTok also disallows models who appear 21 or younger; use clearly older adult talent and retain releases.

## TikTok checks

**Commercial disclosure.** TikTok instructs creators promoting their own business or a third party to enable the content-disclosure setting; the resulting labels distinguish promotional content from paid partnerships. Use the setting appropriate to the actual arrangement and retain a review screenshot. [TikTok: Promoting a brand, product, or service](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service).

**AI disclosure.** Use TikTok’s AI-generated-content label when required for realistic AI imagery, audio, or video. Also keep the plain-language fictional-demo label so viewers understand product examples. Disclosures do not make fabricated public-figure endorsements acceptable. [TikTok: AI-generated content](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content).

**Dating advertisements.** TikTok’s adult-content advertising policy restricts permitted dating ads to adults 18+, requires work with a TikTok Sales Representative, excludes specified markets, and bars sexually inappropriate or purely casual-sex promotion. It also disallows negative portrayals of single life and models who appear 21 or younger. Review both the creative and landing page before requesting paid distribution. Organic eligibility does not imply paid-ad approval. [TikTok Advertising Policies: Adult content, updated August 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-adult-content).

**Music.** Choose music in TikTok’s Commercial Music Library for the campaign’s region and placement, or obtain a separate license covering the intended use. A sound available to a personal account is not evidence of commercial rights. [TikTok: How to use the Commercial Music Library, updated July 2026](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library).

## Final production check

Before a person publishes: confirm the current product state, clear the media and music rights, check the disclosure setting and captions, verify that demo labels persist, confirm the CTA works on a phone, and ensure the post promises only the current founding beta. Have the founder approve product facts and the account owner approve publication. This implementation prepares materials; it does not authorize contacting creators or publishing on the user’s behalf.
